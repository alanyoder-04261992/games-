Broadside

Two admirals, two hidden fleets. Sink every enemy ship before yours go down.

1. Position your fleet

Each admiral places their ships on their own grid, out of sight of the other. Ships lie straight, across or down, and can't overlap or hang off the edge. They may touch.

2. Trade shots

Take turns calling a square in enemy waters. A white marker means a miss; a red marker and flames mean a hit. When every square of a ship has been hit, it sinks and everyone sees which ship it was.

Firing modes

  • One shot a turn: the standard game.
  • Chain fire: every hit earns another shot right away.
  • Barrage: one shot for each of your ships still afloat, so losing ships costs you firepower.

3. Win

Sink the whole enemy fleet.

On screen

  • Placing: pick a ship at the bottom, then tap your grid. Tap a placed ship to pick it back up. ⟳ (or the R key) turns it; Shuffle scatters your fleet at random.
  • Firing: on your turn the camera swings to enemy waters. Tap a square to fire.
  • The mini-map in the corner shows the grid you aren't looking at; tap it to switch views.
  • Computer admirals: Easy fires mostly at random, Normal hunts down ships it has hit, and Expert works out where your remaining ships can still fit.
