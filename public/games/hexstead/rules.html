Hexstead

Settle a hex island. Harvest resources, trade, and build until you reach the target score (10 points unless the table chose otherwise). You must reach it on your own turn to win.

The island

  • Forest gives Timber, Clay Pits give Brick, Pasture gives Wool, Fields give Grain, and the Quarry gives Stone. The Badlands give nothing.
  • Each tile has a number token. The dots under the number show how often it rolls: 6 and 8 (in red) come up most, 2 and 12 least.

Getting started

Everyone places a homestead and a trail, then the same again in reverse order (the last player goes twice in a row). Your second homestead hands you one of each resource around it.

Your turn

  1. Roll the dice. Every tile with that number produces: each homestead touching it collects 1 card, each barn collects 2.
  2. Trade. Offer a trade to the table, or trade with the bank at 4 of one kind for 1 of any other. A harbor you have built on improves that: 3:1 harbors take 3 of anything, 2:1 harbors take 2 of their resource.
  3. Build as much as you can afford, then end your turn.

  • Trails run along tile edges and must connect to your other trails or homesteads. You can't run a trail through someone else's homestead.
  • Homesteads go on corners touching one of your trails, and never right next to another homestead (there must be at least two edges between them).
  • You have 15 trails, 5 homesteads and 4 barns. Building a barn frees up the homestead it replaces.

Sevens and the bandit

When a 7 is rolled, nobody collects. Anyone holding more than 7 resource cards discards half (rounded down). Then the roller moves the bandit to another tile and takes a random card from someone with a building there. The tile under the bandit produces nothing until it moves again. With the Friendly bandit option, the bandit leaves players with 2 points or fewer alone.

Almanac cards

  • Sheriff (14): move the bandit and take a card. Can be played before you roll.
  • Trailblazer (2): build two trails for free.
  • Bumper Crop (2): take any two resources from the bank.
  • Corner the Market (2): name a resource; everyone gives you all of theirs.
  • Landmark (5): worth 1 point, kept secret until the end.

You may play one almanac card per turn, and not on the turn you bought it.

Awards (2 points each)

  • Longest Trail: the first player with an unbroken run of 5 or more trails. Someone has to build a longer run to take it away.
  • Big Posse: the first player to play 3 Sheriffs. Someone has to play more to take it away.

Playing on screen

  • Drag to turn the island, pinch or scroll to zoom. The circular arrow button resets the view.
  • Choose Trail, Homestead or Barn, then tap a glowing spot on the board.
  • Tap an almanac card in your hand to play it.
  • Settings has a low-graphics mode for older devices, and an option to turn down trade offers from computer players automatically.
