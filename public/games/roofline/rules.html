Roofline

You're a roofing crew racing to shingle a 5 × 5 shed roof. Take bundles of shingles from the lumber yard, stack them on your staging racks, and nail them down at the end of each round. Neat roofs with long runs score best.

The yard

Each round, pallets are loaded with 4 bundles from the bag (5 pallets for 2 players, 7 for 3, 9 for 4). There are 20 bundles of each color: Cedar, Slate, Barn Red, Forest and Sand.

Your turn

Take every bundle of one color from either

  • one pallet — the other bundles on it slide into the yard, or
  • the yard. The first roofer to take from the yard each round also takes the foreman's whistle 📣: they start the next round, but the whistle sits in one of their gutter slots.

Put the bundles on one of your five racks. Rack 1 holds 1 bundle, rack 5 holds 5. A rack holds one color at a time, and can't take a color that its roof row already has. Bundles that don't fit fall in the gutter (you can also drop them straight in).

End of the round

When the yard and every pallet are empty, each full rack nails one shingle onto its roof row, in the square of that color, and the rest of the rack goes to the scrap bin. Racks that aren't full keep their bundles for next round.

  • A shingle with no neighbours scores 1.
  • Otherwise it scores the length of the run it joins across, plus the length of the run it joins down.
  • With dormers on, three ★ squares (the same on every roof) score +2 more.
  • The gutter costs −1, −1, −2, −2, −2, −3, −3 points for its slots. Your score never drops below 0.

Game end

The game ends after the round in which anyone completes a whole roof row. Then roofs earn bonuses:

Each complete…Bonus
Row+2
Column+5
Color (all 5 of it on the roof)+8

Most points wins. On a tie, the roofer with more complete rows wins.

On screen

  • Tap a bundle on a pallet or in the yard to pick up all of that color there. The racks that can take it light up, and the squares it would fill on your roof pulse. Tap a rack, or the gutter. Tap the bundle again (or press Esc) to put it back.
  • Computer roofers: Easy often grabs what's handy, Normal plays for the points in front of it, and Expert also counts what's left in the yard and plans for the roof bonuses.
