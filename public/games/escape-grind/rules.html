Escape the Grind

Everyone starts in a job, living paycheck to paycheck on the inner loop — the Grind. Your goal is to buy things that pay you every month until that passive income is bigger than all of your monthly expenses. Then you break out to the outer loop — the Freedom Ring — and race to win.

Your money

  • Cash flow = salary + passive income − expenses. You collect it every time you pass or land on a green Payday space.
  • Passive income comes from rentals, shed lots, storage, businesses — anything with a monthly cash flow.
  • Open your financial statement (the page button, or Y on a controller) any time to see your income, expenses, assets and debts. Tap a player at the top to see theirs.

Spaces on the Grind

  • Opportunity — pick a Small Deal (up to $5,000 down: starter houses, condos, shed lots, stocks, coins) or a Big Deal (duplexes, apartments, storage, shed dealerships, businesses). Buy it or pass.
  • Market — a buyer makes an offer. Anyone who owns a matching asset may sell. You keep the price minus the loan.
  • Splurge — you bought something you didn't need. Pay up.
  • Give — give 10% of your income and roll one or two dice for your next three turns.
  • New Baby — congratulations! Expenses go up (up to three children).
  • Laid Off — pay one month of expenses and sit out two turns.

Stocks

Stock cards let the player who drew them buy any number of shares or sell what they own at that price. Stocks pay no monthly income — buy low, sell high.

Loans

Before you roll you can pay off a debt (it removes that monthly payment) or borrow from the bank in $1,000 steps. Bank loans cost 10% a month. If you can't pay a bill, the bank lends you the difference automatically.

The Freedom Ring

When passive income beats expenses you break free. Your new freedom income is ten times your passive income plus $20,000, paid on every Freedom Payday. Roll two dice each turn.

  • Venture — buy a large business that adds to your freedom income.
  • Dream — buy the dream on that space to win.
  • Setback — lawsuits, audits and storms cost you cash.

You also win by adding $50,000 a month of new freedom income. If the round limit is reached, the player furthest along wins.

Real-life economy (advanced)

Pick Economy: Real-life on the setup page. A market index moves every round through a business cycle, just like the real world: Expansion → Boom → Slowdown → Recession → Recovery. The gauge on the left shows where you are, with a price chart.

  • Deal prices follow the economy. In a recession houses, shed lots and businesses go on sale — often 20–30% off — while the rent they pay stays the same. In a boom everything costs more.
  • Buyers follow it too. Market offers pay more in a boom and less in a recession.
  • Business income swings. Small businesses earn more in a boom and about a quarter less in a recession. Rentals and storage hold steady.
  • Loan rates move. Borrowing is cheap in a recession and expensive in a boom.
  • A live stock market. Open Stock market before you roll to buy or sell at today's price. Jumpy stocks (NPK, OAK) swing hardest; the fund (BMF) and the railroad (CRL) move gently.

Making money in a downturn

  • Save cash in good times. The players who win a recession are the ones with cash when prices fall.
  • Buy when everything is on sale. A rental bought 25% off pays the same rent, so your return is much higher.
  • Buy the dip, sell the boom. Buy stocks in a recession and sell them when the economy is booming.
  • Short selling. When you expect a stock to fall, short it: you profit by how far the price drops, and you lose if it rises. You must keep cash equal to half of every short; if the loss gets too big the bank closes it for you (a margin call). Cover your shorts once the fall has happened.
  • Sell into a boom. When Market buyers pay top dollar, sell and buy again when prices come back down.

Controls

  • Mouse / touch — drag to orbit the table, scroll or pinch to zoom, tap buttons. Lost the board? Tap the target button or double-tap the table to center it again.
  • Game controller — A choose, B back / pass, D-pad or left stick to move between buttons, Y statement, X camera angle, right stick orbit (click it to center the board), LT/RT zoom, Start full screen, View/Select flips through players' statements.
  • Keyboard — arrows and Enter, Esc back, S statement, C camera, R center the board, F full screen.

All jobs, companies, deals and numbers in this game are made up.
