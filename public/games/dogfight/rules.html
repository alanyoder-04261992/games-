Coastal Dogfight

Fly a 1940s propeller fighter over a 20 km stretch of coast. Pick your aircraft, then take off from the runway or launch straight into the air.

Modes

  • Co-op vs raiders. Everyone at the table flies on the same side against waves of raiders coming in off the sea. The table options set how many raiders come in each wave and how many waves there are; the last wave brings an ace. When the sky is clear, the host lands at the airfield to finish the sortie.
  • Free-for-all. Every pilot for themselves. The first to the kill target wins the round.

If you are shot down you are back in the air after six seconds. Computer pilots fly the empty seats in a free-for-all, and in co-op when Computer wingmen is on. Their skill follows the table's computer player setting.

Difficulty, missiles and flares

  • Difficulty sets how good the raiders are, how much damage you take, and who carries missiles. Easy is forgiving. Normal arms the ace. Hard arms the veterans too. On Ace every raider carries two.
  • Missiles: keep a target near your nose and a square closes in with a growling tone. When it flashes red and the tone goes steady, fire (MSL, X or right click). Auto-lock uses a wide cone; switch to manual in the settings for a harder, centred lock. Land and stop on the airfield to rearm.
  • Flares: when the missile warning beeps, break hard and drop flares (FLARE or F). Each pair has a good chance of pulling the missile away.
  • Boost: hold Shift at full throttle (or drag into the red top of the slider) for about 18 seconds of extra power. It recharges when you ease off.
  • Which way is up: the round dial by the flight data is an artificial horizon. Blue is sky, brown is earth, and it reads INVERTED when you're upside down. Far out the sea is a darker grey-blue than the sky, so the horizon shows as a line.
  • Corner map: the radar, a map of the whole sector, or off (MAP or M).

Flying

  • Phone: left thumb steers, the slider sets the throttle, hold FIRE. The assisted stick levels the wings and holds your height when you let go. Turn Phone mode on or off in the game's menu.
  • Desktop: move the mouse to aim, click or Space to fire, Shift and Z for throttle, G for gear, V for flaps, C for the cockpit view, Esc for settings. W A S D take direct control.
  • Gamepad: left stick to fly, RT to fire, A for gear, B for camera.

Tips

  • Aim at the small diamond in front of a target. It shows where your rounds will meet it.
  • Green brackets are friends, red are enemies. The radar shows both.
  • Keep your speed up in a fight. Pull too hard and the stall horn sounds, and the wing drops.
  • Pausing stops the flight, also at a table where only computer pilots fly with you. When another person is flying at your table the round can't stop, so it carries on and your aircraft holds level flight until you resume.
