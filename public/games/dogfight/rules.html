Coastal Dogfight

Fly a 1940s propeller fighter over a 20 km stretch of coast. Pick your aircraft, then take off from the runway or launch straight into the air.

Modes

  • Co-op · free play and missions. Everyone flies on the same side. The round starts in free play: no raiders, just the sky. Anyone can start a mission (Dogfight, Naval strike, Ground attack, Train busting or Bomber intercept) from the menu before launching or from the pause menu while flying. The others see a Join mission banner at the top. Tap it to join, even partway through, or tap ✕ to stay in free play. You can also start a different mission of your own, so several can run at once. Anyone's hits count, and you get the points for what you destroy. When a mission is won, everyone in it gets +500 and goes back to free play. The host ends the round from the pause menu.
  • Free-for-all. Every pilot for themselves. The first to the kill target wins the round.

If you are shot down you are back in the air after six seconds. Computer pilots fly the empty seats in a free-for-all, and in co-op when Computer wingmen is on. Their skill follows the table's computer player setting.

Campaign

Playing alone, the menu's Campaign tab runs ten missions in a row, by day, at sunset and at night, ending in a duel with the Red Wasp. Each earns up to three stars: finish it, don't lose an aircraft, fly it to an A. Finishing missions unlocks aircraft for the campaign, and stars earn medals. Free flight & missions keeps every aircraft and mission open.

Difficulty, missiles and flares

  • Difficulty sets how good the raiders are, how much damage you take, and who carries missiles. Easy is forgiving. Normal arms the ace. Hard arms the veterans too. On Ace every raider carries two.
  • Missiles: keep a target near your nose and a square closes in with a growling tone. When it flashes red and the tone goes steady, fire (MSL, X or right click). Auto-lock uses a wide cone; switch to manual in the settings for a harder, centred lock. Land and stop on the airfield to rearm.
  • Flares: when the missile warning beeps, break hard and drop flares (FLARE or F). Each pair has a good chance of pulling the missile away.
  • Boost: hold Shift at full throttle (or drag into the red top of the slider) for about 18 seconds of extra power. It recharges when you ease off.
  • Switch mission mid-flight: pause, pick a mission and tap Start it here, now. Every aircraft carries bombs, in the dogfight too (B, BOMB or LT).
  • Tilt: choose Tilt phone in the pause menu. On iPhone the first tap asks for motion access; tap the left half of the screen any time to re-centre.
  • Missions: Dogfight, Naval strike (sink the battleship), Ground attack (destroy the convoy and fuel depot), Train busting (stop the ammunition train before it reaches the harbour) or Bomber intercept (stop the bombers before three reach the town). On attack missions drop bombs with B in the air, BOMB on touch or LT on a gamepad; the gold circle on screen shows where they will land. Flak bursts near you hurt, so keep moving.
  • Aircraft: five 1940s fighters (Kestrel, Falke, Ranger, Swift, Thunder), the Brute bomber, two modern jets (Lancer, Talon) and the Striker attack jet. The raiders fly the other side's aircraft of your era. A jet's boost is its afterburner: it doesn't run out but burns fuel fast. Jets carry a rotary cannon, two more missiles and more flares, and their fly-by-wire stops a hard pull at 9 g.
  • Which way is up: the round dial by the flight data is an artificial horizon. Blue is sky, brown is earth, and it reads INVERTED when you're upside down. Far out the sea is a darker grey-blue than the sky, so the horizon shows as a line.
  • Rockets: K, RKT or L3 fires a pair. Great against the ammunition train: a wagon going up can set off the next one.
  • Wingmen: T attack my target, Y cover me, U hit the ground target (WING on a phone or iPad).
  • Bail out: when you are shot down, press J (BAIL, R3) before the wreck hits. You float down, get picked up, and go up again in a fresh aircraft.
  • Named aces lead the last wave: they break hard when you sit on their tail and make a last stand when badly hit. +1000.
  • Night: searchlights sweep the sky round the targets. Caught in one, the flak finds you; turn hard to break out.
  • Corner map: the radar, a map of the whole sector, or off (MAP or M).

Flying

  • iPad: touch controls come on by themselves, larger and spread out, with a bigger HUD.
  • Phone: left thumb steers, the slider sets the throttle, hold FIRE. The assisted stick levels the wings and holds your height when you let go. Turn Phone mode on or off in the game's menu. Turn on Rudder buttons there for ◀ RUD and RUD ▶: hold one to swing the nose that way.
  • Desktop: move the mouse to aim, click or Space to fire, Shift and Z for throttle, G for gear, V for flaps, C for the cockpit view, Esc for settings. W A S D take direct control.
  • Gamepad: left stick to fly, RT to fire, A for gear, B for camera.

Tips

  • Aim at the small diamond in front of a target. It shows where your rounds will meet it.
  • Green brackets are friends, red are enemies. The radar shows both.
  • Keep your speed up in a fight. Pull too hard and the stall horn sounds, and the wing drops.
  • Pausing stops the flight, also at a table where only computer pilots fly with you. When another person is flying at your table the round can't stop, so it carries on and your aircraft holds level flight until you resume.
