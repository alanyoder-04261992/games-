Spit & Speed

Two card games for two players with one deck — and no turns. You both play at the same time, as fast as you can. Pick the game on the setup page.

Playing a card

  • A card can go on a center pile if it is one higher or one lower than the top card, whatever the suit: a 7 goes on a 6 or an 8.
  • Ace and King wrap around (K–A–2), unless the table rule says “Ace is low only.”
  • Tap a card to play it — it goes on the pile that leaves you the most moves — or drag it to the pile you want. On a keyboard, press 1–5.
  • If you both go for the same pile, the first card down wins. The other one is “too slow” and stays in your hand.

Speed

  • Each player has a hand of 5 cards and a draw pile of 15. Two side piles of 6 sit beside the center.
  • Both press Speed! to turn up one side card onto each center pile, then play.
  • Your hand refills from your draw pile by itself.
  • When neither of you can play, press Speed! again to flip two new cards. If the side piles are gone, the cards under the center tops are shuffled into new side piles.
  • First to play every card in their hand and draw pile wins.

Spit

  • Each player gets half the deck and lays out five stockpiles of 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 cards, top card face up. The rest is your spit pile.
  • Both press Spit! to spit a card onto the center piles, then play your stockpile tops onto them.
  • When a stockpile's top is played, the card under it turns up. If you have an empty space, tap or drag a top card into it to turn up the card underneath.
  • When nobody can play, spit again. If one of you is out of spit cards, the other spits on both piles.
  • Clear all five stockpiles and it's time to slap: tap a center pile (or press ← / →). Whoever slaps first takes that pile; the other player takes the other one. Slap the smaller pile!
  • Everyone gathers their cards, deals new stockpiles and plays the next round. Clear your stockpiles with no spit pile left and you win.

Playing the computer

The computer's level is how fast its hands are: Easy is slow and makes mistakes, Lightning is quick. The table's game speed setting slows it down or speeds it up too.
