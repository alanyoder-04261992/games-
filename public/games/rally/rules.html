Ridgeline Rally

Race country roads over hills, farms and lakeshores. Everyone at the table drives their own car on the same circuit, with computer cars filling out the field if the host wants them.

The race

  • The host picks the circuit, the number of laps, how many computer cars join and how good they are. Everyone picks their own car and colour, then the host starts the race for the whole table.
  • Five red lights come on one by one. When they go out, go.
  • A lap only counts if you go all the way round. Cutting across the fields gets you reset onto the road.
  • The race ends when every driver has finished, or 45 seconds after the first car crosses the line. Anyone still on track is classified where they are.

The cars

  • Sparrow RS: a light front-wheel-drive hatch. Easy to drive and quick to turn.
  • Brumby V8: a heavy rear-wheel-drive coupe with the most power. It slides if you stamp on the gas in a corner.
  • Vela GT: a mid-engine car with downforce. The fastest through quick corners.

Driving

  • Phone: slide your left thumb to steer, hold GAS and BRAKE on the right, DRIFT for the handbrake. You can switch to left and right buttons or tilt steering. Turn Phone mode on or off in the game's menu.
  • Desktop: W A S D or the arrow keys, Space for the handbrake, R to put the car back on the road, C for the camera, M for the map, Esc for the menu.
  • Gamepad: left stick to steer, RT gas, LT brake, A handbrake, X reset, Y camera.
  • Hold brake when stopped to reverse.
  • Drifting: going into a corner, tap the handbrake (DRIFT, Space or A) while you steer. The back of the car slides out and the slide holds by itself: keep steering into the turn for more angle and stay on the gas to keep your speed. Let go of the steering and it settles; steer the other way to straighten up. The car will not spin round.
  • The brakes have ABS, so you can keep steering while you brake hard into a corner.

Tips

  • Driving assist counter-steers slides and eases the gas when the wheels spin. It leaves a drift you start with the handbrake alone. Auto accelerate holds the gas for you so you only steer and brake. Both are in the settings.
  • The red and white boards on the roadside warn of a big braking zone ahead.
  • Off the tarmac there is less grip and more drag. Trees stop you dead.
  • The game never puts you back on the road by itself. If you end up far off it, or stuck in a field, tap BACK TO THE ROAD (or press R, or RESET on a phone).
  • Graphics in the settings: High has shadows, soft contact shading, sun glow and the most trees and grass; choose Medium or Low if a phone or older computer runs slowly.
  • Playing alone, try Time trial: your best lap is saved as a ghost car to race next time.
