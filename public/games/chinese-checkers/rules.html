How to play Chinese Checkers

The goal

Your marbles start in one point of the star. Move all of them into the point straight across the board. The first player to fill that point wins.

Players

  • 2 players start in opposite points.
  • 3 players start in every other point, so each one races into an empty point.
  • 4 players start in two pairs of opposite points.
  • 6 players fill the whole star.

Empty seats are filled by computer players. Turns go round clockwise.

Your turn

Move one marble, one of two ways:

  • Step into an empty hole right next to it.
  • Jump over one marble next to it (anyone's, yours too) into the empty hole straight beyond. After a jump you may keep jumping from where you landed, in any direction, as many times as you like. You can stop anywhere along the way.

You can't step and jump in the same turn. Nothing is captured: the marbles you jump stay where they are.

Tap a marble to pick it up. Every hole it can reach lights up. Point at a hole to see the jumps on the way, then tap it to move. Tap the marble again (or press Esc) to put it back.

A few more rules

  • Once a marble reaches your target point it may move around inside it, but it can't leave.
  • No blocking: if someone leaves marbles sitting in your target point, you still win once every hole there is filled and at least one of the marbles is yours.
  • If none of your marbles can move, you pass.

Table options

  • Marbles: 10 each is the standard game. 6 each fills only the three rows nearest each tip, for a quicker race.
  • Game ends: with 3 or more players, the game can stop as soon as someone gets home, or play on so everyone finishes 2nd, 3rd and so on.

Tips

  • Long jump ladders win games. Line your marbles up with one-hole gaps so they can leapfrog each other.
  • Don't leave a marble behind at home: the last straggler often decides the race.
  • Watch what your move opens up for the next player.

Settings

Switch between the 3D board and the flat board under Settings (the gear). On the 3D board, drag empty space to turn the view and pinch or scroll to zoom. Your own point is always at the bottom.
