Chinese Checkers

Race your marbles across a star-shaped board by stepping and hopping, and be the first to fill the point on the far side.

  • Players2, 3, 4 or 6 (1 against the computer)
  • Time15–40 minutes
  • Ages7 and up
  • Learn5 minutes
  • Pass and playYes

Goal of the game

All your marbles start in one point of the star. Move every one of them into the point straight across the board. The first player to fill that far point wins.

What’s in the game

  • The board: a wooden six-pointed star with 121 holes.
  • Marbles: 10 for each player (6 in the quick game), one colour per player: Red, Blue, Green, Gold, Purple and White.

The point you start in is your home point. The point straight across from it is your goal (the target point). On the board your home point is tinted in your colour and your goal has a dashed outline in your colour.

Setup

Each player’s marbles fill their home point. Which points are used depends on the number of players:

  • 2 players start in opposite points and race straight past each other.
  • 3 players start in every other point, so each goal starts empty.
  • 4 players use two pairs of opposite points, so each goal starts full of the opponent’s marbles. Nobody starts in or aims for the other two points.
  • 6 players fill the whole star: everyone’s goal is somebody’s home.

The app picks at random who moves first. After that, turns go round the star clockwise.

How a turn works

On your turn you move one marble, in one of two ways: a step or one or more hops.

  1. Pick one of your marbles. Any of them, as long as it has somewhere to go.
  2. Step or hop. Either step into a touching empty hole, or hop over a touching marble into the empty hole right behind it.
  3. Keep hopping if you like. After a hop you may hop again with the same marble, as often as you can, and stop whenever you want. A step always ends your move.
  4. Next player. Play passes clockwise to the next player still in the game.

Steps

Hops

Chain hops

Hops are what make Chinese Checkers fast. One marble can make a whole chain of hops in a single turn:

  • Each hop jumps exactly one marble that is right next to yours, into the empty hole straight beyond.
  • Each hop can go in any of the six directions, so a chain can zigzag.
  • You can jump any marble: yours or another player’s. Nobody’s marble is ever captured or moved.
  • You may stop after any hop. You don’t have to take every hop on offer.
  • A chain can’t end in the hole it started from.

What isn’t allowed

You can’t mix the two kinds of move. A step can’t be followed by a hop, and a hop can’t be followed by a step: once you have hopped, you can only hop again or stop.

More rules: goal, passing and the end

Once home, stay home

A marble that is already inside your goal point may still move around inside it (to make room for the others), but its move must end inside the goal. It can’t leave again.

During a chain of hops a marble in your goal may pass through holes outside it, as long as the last hop lands back inside the goal.

Other points

Marbles may move through, and stop in, any point on the way, including other players’ home points and empty points.

Some rule sets forbid stopping in a point that is neither your home nor your goal. This version doesn’t: the whole star is open.

No blocking by staying at home

You win when every hole of your goal point is filled and at least one of the marbles there is yours. So a player who leaves marbles sitting in your goal can’t stop you: fill all the other holes and you win.

Passing

If none of your marbles can move, you must pass. The app shows a Pass button. You can’t pass while you still have a move.

The end of the game

  • With 2 players, the game ends as soon as one player fills their goal. That player wins.
  • With 3 or more players, it depends on the Game ends option. By default the game ends when the first player gets home, and everyone else is ranked by how far their marbles still have to go. With Play on for 2nd, 3rd… the finished players drop out and the rest keep racing until only one player is left.
  • Time limit: if a game drags on for 220 turns each without a winner, it stops, and the player whose marbles have the least distance left wins (“furthest along when time ran out”); anyone already home keeps their place. Normal games come nowhere near this.

Example turn

It is Red’s turn. Red taps the marble at the bottom of the picture.

  1. The choices light up. The marble can step into any of the touching empty holes, or start hopping: over the blue marble up and to the right, then on up the board.
  2. Red points at the farthest glowing hole. A dotted line shows the route: three hops, up-right, up-left, up-right, with a small dot at each landing.
  3. Red taps that hole. The marble hops along the route, landing with a click each time, and stops six rows further up the board. One move, three hops: that is how races are won.

Table options

  • Players: 2 (default), 3, 4 or 6. Seats without a person get computer players.
  • Marbles: 10 each (standard), the default, fill all four rows of each point. 6 each (quick game) fill only the three rows nearest the tip, and your goal is just those 6 holes of the far point.
  • Game ends (3+ players): When the first player gets home (default), or Play on for 2nd, 3rd… With 2 players the game always ends when the first player gets home.
  • Computer players: Easy, Normal (default) or Expert.
    • Easy likes moving forward but often picks a so-so move.
    • Normal takes the move that gains the most ground right now, while making sure no marble gets left far behind.
    • Expert also looks at its own best follow-up move, so it builds ladders, and avoids moves that hand the next player a long jump.
  • Game speed: Relaxed, Normal (default), Fast or Turbo: how quickly computer players move.

Playing in the Game Center

Moving a marble

  1. Tap one of your marbles. On your turn, the marbles that can move have a soft glowing ring. The one you tap gets a bright ring and every hole it can reach lights up. A marble with nowhere to go says it is boxed in.
  2. Look before you leap. With a mouse, point at a lit hole to see the route of hops on the way.
  3. Tap a lit hole to move there. The app finds the hops for you.
  4. Changed your mind? Tap the marble again, tap an empty hole that isn’t lit, or press Esc to put it back. Tap a different marble of yours to pick that one instead.

The last move stays marked with a dotted line along its hops and a ring where it started, so you can see what the previous player did. The players at the top show whose turn it is and how many marbles each has home (for example “7/10 home”).

The board

  • Your point is always at the bottom. The board turns so you play “up the screen”, whichever seat you are in.
  • 3D board (on by default): tap marbles and holes as above. Drag to turn the view (it drifts back after a while); pinch or use the mouse wheel to zoom.
  • Flat board: switch the 3D board off under Settings (the gear) → 3D board. The choice is remembered on this device, and the flat board is used automatically if the device can’t show 3D graphics. On the flat board you can also use the keyboard: Tab moves between the marbles and holes you can use, Enter or Space picks one.

Players and tables

  • Single player: you against computer players. The game is saved on this device; the game’s page offers Continue your saved game.
  • Multiplayer → Own phones (online): share the table code or invite link. Empty seats are filled by computer players when the host starts. Online tables have chat and voice chat.
  • Multiplayer → This device (pass and play): type a name for each person playing on this device (leave a name empty for a computer player) and hand the device to whoever’s turn it is. Nothing is hidden, so there is no cover screen.
  • When the game ends, Rematch starts again with the same players and seats (Play again against the computer). With Play on, a message announces each player who gets home and their place.

Tips and strategy

1. Hop, don’t walk

A step gains one hole. A hop gains two, and a chain of hops can cross half the board. Look for hops first, every turn. When nothing hops, step a marble to where it will make a hop possible next turn.

2. Build ladders

Line marbles up in straight lines with one empty hole between each. Then a marble from the back can climb the whole ladder in one move, and the marble that just arrived becomes a new rung at the front. Moving your marbles as a connected group that keeps feeding ladders forward is the heart of good play.

3. Use everyone’s marbles

Any marble can be a rung, including your opponents’. In the middle of the board, the other players’ marbles often give you the longest chains. Check every lit hole before you settle for a short move.

4. Don’t build their ladder

Before you move, look at what your marble does for the next player. A marble left one hole away from theirs, in a straight line towards their goal, is a free rung for them.

5. Block when it is cheap

A marble sitting in a landing hole breaks a ladder. If a move that helps you also lands in the hole an opponent was about to hop into, take it. Don’t waste many moves on blocking, though: it is a race, and every move spent going sideways is a move not spent going forward.

6. Leave no one behind

The last marble out of your home point often decides the game: it has the whole board to cross alone, with nobody left to hop over. Bring your back marbles along with the group.

7. Fill your goal from the tip

Send the first marbles that arrive deep into the far tip of your goal, so later ones have room behind them. A goal clogged at its mouth forces slow single steps at the very end.

8. Middle game: go straight

Your goal is straight across. Marbles drifting off to the side corners waste moves. Use sideways steps only to set up a hop.

Quick reference

Goal

Fill the point opposite your home with your marbles. First to do it wins.

Your turn

Move one marble: a step to a touching empty hole, or one or more hops. If nothing can move, pass.

Hops

Over one touching marble (anyone’s) into the empty hole beyond. Chain as many as you like, any direction, stop anytime. Nothing is captured.

Goal rules

A marble in your goal can’t leave it. You win when every goal hole is full and at least one holds your marble.

Players

2, 3, 4 or 6. Random first player, then clockwise. 10 marbles each, or 6 in the quick game.

Controls

Tap a marble, then a lit hole. Tap it again or Esc to put it back.

Glossary

Point
One of the six triangles of the star, 10 holes each.
Home point
The point your marbles start in.
Goal (target point)
The point straight across from your home. Fill it to win.
Step
Moving a marble into a touching empty hole. It ends your move.
Hop (jump)
Moving a marble over one touching marble into the empty hole straight beyond it.
Chain
Several hops in a row by the same marble in one turn.
Ladder
Marbles spaced one hole apart in a line, so another marble can hop along them.
Rung
One marble in a ladder: the marble you hop over.
Blocking
Putting a marble in a hole an opponent wants to hop into, breaking their ladder.
Boxed in
A marble with no step and no hop available.
Pass
Skipping your turn, allowed only when none of your marbles can move.
