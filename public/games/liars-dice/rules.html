Liar's Dice

Everyone has a cup of dice that only they can see. Bid on what's under all the cups together, call out a bluff, and be the last player with dice left.

1. Roll

Each round, every player shakes their cup and peeks at their own dice. Everyone starts with the same number of dice (5 unless the table chose otherwise).

2. Bid

A bid is a guess about every die on the table: "7 × four" means "there are at least seven fours under all the cups put together". Going around the table, each player must either raise the bid or call it.

A raise is either

  • a bigger number on any face (7 fours → 8 twos), or
  • the same number on a higher face (7 fours → 7 fives).

Wild ones (on by default): every 1 counts as whatever face was bid, so nobody bids on ones.

3. Call

  • Liar! You think the last bid is too high. Everyone lifts their cup and the dice are counted. If there are fewer than the bid said, the bidder loses a die. If the bid was good, you lose one.
  • Spot on (if the table allows it): you think the bid is exactly right. If it is, every other player loses a die. If it isn't, you lose one.

Whoever lost a die starts the next round. Run out of dice and you're out. The last player with dice wins.

On screen

  • On your turn, set the number with − and +, tap a face, then Bid. Or tap Liar! or Spot on.
  • Keyboard: ↑ ↓ change the number, ← → or 1–6 pick a face, Enter bids, L calls liar.
  • Show the odds (in Settings) gives the chance a bid is true, worked out from your own dice only. It can't see anyone else's cup, and it doesn't guess what the other players' bids give away.
  • Computer players: Easy goes mostly by its own dice and bluffs a lot, Normal starts to read the other bids, and Expert reads the table closely and picks its calls carefully.
