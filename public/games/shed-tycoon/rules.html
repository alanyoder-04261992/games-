Shed Tycoon

Buy lumber, steel, glass, paint and shingles, build sheds, barns and garages, and grow a backyard business until the big jobs cost almost nothing.

  • Players1–4 (2–4 builders; computers fill empty seats)
  • Lengthplay to 12, 15 or 18 points
  • DifficultyEasy rules, deep planning
  • Pass and playYes, with a cover between turns

Goal of the game

You run a backyard building company. Each turn you either take materials or use them to build an order from the board. Every building you finish gives you a permanent discount of one material, so your yard keeps getting cheaper to build from.

Buildings and sales lots give points. When someone reaches the target score (15 by default), the round is finished and the builder with the most points wins.

Sales lots

There are 20 sales lots. Each wants a yard with enough buildings of certain discount colours: 4 each of two colours (10 lots) or 3 each of three colours (10 lots). Every lot is worth 3 points.

Setup

  1. The supply. Put out 4, 5 or 7 of each material (for 2, 3 or 4 players) and 5 cash.
  2. The order board. Shuffle each deck and lay out 4 face-up orders from it: garages & lodges on top, barns in the middle, sheds at the bottom.
  3. The sales lots. Lay out one more lot than there are players.
  4. Builders. Everyone starts with no tokens, no buildings and no points. A random player goes first.

How a turn works

On your turn you do exactly one of these four things:

  1. Take 3 different materials from the supply. If fewer than three kinds are left, take one of each kind that is left.
  2. Take 2 of the same material, but only if there are at least 4 of it in the supply before you take.
  3. Hold an order: take a face-up order, or the top order of a deck without looking (a blind hold), into your hand of held orders, and take 1 cash if there is any. You can hold at most 3.
  4. Build a face-up order or one you are holding: pay its cost, less your discounts. See Building.

Then, at the end of your turn:

  1. Too many tokens? If you have more than 10 tokens (cash included), hand back tokens of your choice until you have 10.
  2. Sales lot. If your buildings meet a lot's needs, that lot signs on with you: +3 points. At most one lot per turn.
  3. Target reached? If you now have the target score or more, this is the final round.
You can't take cash from the supply directly; it only comes with holding an order. If no move at all is possible, you pass.

Taking materials

Taken tokens go into your yard. You must take a full set: three different (or every kind left, if fewer than three kinds remain), or two of one. You can't take just one or two different materials while three kinds are still available.

Holding orders

Holding an order keeps it safe for you: no one else can build it. It also gives you 1 cash (if the supply has any left), and cash is the only wild token.

  • Face up: take any order from the board. The gap is refilled at once from that deck, if it has any orders left.
  • Blind: take the top order of a deck without it ever being on the board. Only you can see it; the others see a face-down card with its tier's dots until the game ends.
  • You can hold up to 3 orders. Held orders stay in your hand until you build them; you can't hand them back.
Hold an order when a rival is about to build it, when you need cash to finish a build next turn, or to grab a big lodge you will build later.

Building

To build, choose a face-up order or one you are holding and pay for it:

  1. Take off your discounts. For each material, every building you already own of that colour cuts the cost by 1 (not below 0).
  2. Pay the rest with materials. Use your tokens of each material first.
  3. Fill any gap with cash. Each cash token pays for one missing material of any kind. If you can't cover the cost this way, you can't build it.
  4. Take the building. Paid tokens go back to the supply. The order goes into your yard: its points count and its discount works from your next build on. A face-up gap is refilled from the deck.

Your yard and your discounts

Discounts make big buildings cheap. With 3 Steel buildings, the Lake Cabin (7 Steel) costs you only 4 Steel. A building whose whole cost is covered by your discounts is free: you build it without spending a token.

On the screen, orders you can afford right now glow green. Tap any order to see its cost next to what you have.

Sales lots

A sales lot looks only at your buildings, not your tokens. At the end of each of your turns, if your buildings meet a lot's needs, it signs on with you for 3 points and leaves the table. You win at most one lot per turn; if you qualify for two, the other can come at the end of your next turn (if no one beats you to it).

End of the game and scoring

As soon as a builder ends a turn with at least the target score (12, 15 or 18), the final round begins. Play goes on until the turn would come back to the player who went first, so everyone has had the same number of turns. Then the game ends.

Most points wins. If players are tied, the one with fewer buildings wins. If they also have the same number of buildings, they share the win.

If nobody can move for a very long time, the game stops itself after 120 rounds and scores as normal.

Worked example: a shed, start to finish

A two-player game. On the board are the Bike Port (shed, Steel discount: 1 Lumber, 1 Glass, 1 Shingles), the Wood Crib (shed, Lumber discount: 2 Glass, 1 Paint) and the Red Barn (barn, 1 point, Paint discount: 2 Lumber, 3 Steel, 2 Shingles).

  1. Turn 1: take 3 different. Lumber, Glass, Shingles. Tokens: 1 Lumber, 1 Glass, 1 Shingles.
  2. Turn 2: build the Bike Port. Pay 1 Lumber, 1 Glass, 1 Shingles. Tokens: none. Discounts: Steel 1.
  3. Turn 3: take 2 Glass (4 Glass were in the supply). Tokens: 2 Glass.
  4. Turn 4: take 3 different. Lumber, Paint, Shingles. Tokens: 1 Lumber, 2 Glass, 1 Paint, 1 Shingles.
  5. Turn 5: build the Wood Crib. Pay 2 Glass, 1 Paint. Tokens: 1 Lumber, 1 Shingles. Discounts: Lumber 1, Steel 1.
  6. Turn 6: hold the Red Barn. It's safe in your hand, and you take 1 cash. Tokens: 1 Lumber, 1 Shingles, 1 cash.
  7. Turn 7: take 3 different. Steel, Glass, Shingles. Tokens: 1 Lumber, 1 Steel, 1 Glass, 2 Shingles, 1 cash.
  8. Turn 8: build the Red Barn from your hand. Cost 2 Lumber, 3 Steel, 2 Shingles, less discounts 1 Lumber and 1 Steel = 1 Lumber, 2 Steel, 2 Shingles. Pay 1 Lumber, 1 Steel, 1 cash (for the second Steel) and 2 Shingles. Left: 1 Glass. Score: 1 point, and discounts are now Lumber 1, Steel 1, Paint 1.

Eight turns for one point looks slow, but those three discounts now cut every future build. Two zero-point sheds paid for themselves the moment the Red Barn needed two fewer tokens.

Computer builders

  • Easy grabs and builds on impulse and ignores the sales lots.
  • Normal plans for the orders it can reach soonest and gives some weight to the sales lots.
  • Expert plans further ahead, chases the sales lots harder and holds orders when the race gets close.

Playing in the Game Center

  • Take materials: tap materials in the supply to put them in your cart, then press Take. Tap a token in the cart to put it back. The cart only lets you make a legal set.
  • Build or hold: tap any order to open it. It shows the cost, which of your tokens it would use ("You pay: …") and the buttons Build it and Hold it (+1 cash). Orders you can afford glow green.
  • Hold blind: tap a striped deck, then Hold it.
  • Your held orders sit under your yard; tap one to build it.
  • Hand back tokens: when you have more than 10, the status line tells you how many; tap your own materials to hand them back.
  • The status line at the top says whose turn it is and what just happened, and shows "Final round!" when the target has been reached.
  • Pass and play: several people can share one device. Held blind orders are secret, so a "Pass to …" cover hides the screen between turns.
  • Online: play friends online with chat and voice; empty seats are computer builders.
  • After the game: the results table shows buildings, lots and points. See yards lets you look at the final table, and the host can press Play again.
  • Press Esc to close an open order.

Tips and strategy

  1. Build cheap sheds early. A zero-point shed is a discount for the rest of the game; the earlier you get it, the more it saves.
  2. Pick a direction. Look at the big orders and the sales lots, and collect discounts in the colours they need.
  3. Free builds are the best builds. Once your discounts cover an order, it costs nothing but a turn.
  4. Count the supply. Taking two of a kind needs four in the supply; watch for colours that are running out, and take the last ones your rivals need.
  5. Hold to block. If a rival is one turn away from an order that wins them a lot or the game, hold it yourself.
  6. Hold for cash. One cash can be the missing token in a big build; holding is the only way to get it.
  7. Don't stockpile. Ten tokens is the limit, and tokens score nothing. Spend them.
  8. Lots are 3 points each. A yard that fits two lots at once is a strong late-game plan, but you get only one lot per turn.
  9. Watch the tie-break. On equal points, fewer buildings wins: big lodges beat lots of little sheds at the finish.

Quick reference

Your turn: one action

Take 3 different · or take 2 of one (4+ in supply) · or hold an order (+1 cash, max 3 held) · or build.

Building

Cost − your discounts. Pay with matching tokens, then cash for any gap. Each building = 1 permanent discount in its colour.

End of your turn

Hand back down to 10 tokens · one sales lot if you qualify (+3) · reached the target? Final round.

Supply

2 players: 4 of each material · 3 players: 5 · 4 players: 7 · always 5 cash.

Points

Sheds 0–1 · barns 1–3 · garages & lodges 3–5 · sales lots 3.

Winning

Finish the round after someone reaches 12/15/18. Most points; tie: fewer buildings; still tied: shared.

Glossary

Blind hold
Holding the top order of a deck without showing it to anyone.
Build
Pay an order's cost (less discounts) and put it in your yard.
Cash
The gold wild token. It pays for one missing material of any kind and only comes with holding an order.
Discount
Every building you own takes 1 off its colour's material in all your later builds.
Final round
The rest of the round after someone reaches the target score.
Hold
Take an order into your hand (up to 3) so only you can build it.
Order
A building card: a shed, a barn or a garage & lodge.
Sales lot
A 3-point bonus that signs on with the first builder whose buildings meet its needs.
Supply
The shared pile of materials and cash in the middle of the table.
Tier
One of the three decks: sheds, barns, or garages & lodges.
Yard
Your buildings, tokens, held orders and sales lots.
