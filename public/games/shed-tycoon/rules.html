Shed Tycoon

You run a backyard building company. Buy materials, build sheds, barns and garages, and grow a yard so well stocked that the big jobs cost almost nothing. The first builder to the target score sets off the final round.

Materials

Five materials — Lumber, Steel, Glass, Paint and Shingles — plus Cash, which stands in for any of them. The supply has 4 of each material with 2 players, 5 with 3 and 7 with 4, and 5 cash.

The order board

Three decks of orders: small sheds, barns, and garages & lodges. Four of each are face up. Every order shows its points, the material it gives you a discount on, and its cost.

On your turn, do one thing

  • Take 3 different materials from the supply (or as many different ones as are left), or
  • Take 2 of one material, if at least 4 of it are in the supply, or
  • Hold an order: take a face-up order (or the top of a deck, face down) into your hand, and take 1 cash if there is any. You can hold up to 3.
  • Build a face-up order or one you're holding: pay its cost, less your discounts, using cash for anything you're short of.

Each building you own takes 1 off that material in every later cost, for the rest of the game. A building whose discounts cover its whole cost is free.

You can store at most 10 tokens. If you end your turn with more, hand back the extra.

Sales lots

A few sales lots are out (one more than the number of players). Each wants a set of buildings — four each of two kinds, or three each of three kinds. At the end of your turn, if your buildings meet a lot's needs, it signs on with you for 3 points (one lot per turn).

Winning

When a builder reaches the target score (12, 15 or 18), finish the round so everyone has had the same number of turns. Most points wins; on a tie, the builder with fewer buildings wins.

On screen

  • Tap materials in the supply to put them in your cart, then press Take. Tap a token in the cart to put it back.
  • Tap an order to see what it would cost you, then Build it or Hold it. Orders you can afford right now glow green. Tap a deck to hold its top order blind.
  • Each yard shows your discounts (the square counts) above your tokens.
  • Computer builders: Easy grabs and builds on impulse, Normal plans for the orders it can reach soonest, and Expert also chases the sales lots and holds orders when the race gets close.
